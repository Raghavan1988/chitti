# PRD: SignalLoop

## Overview

**SignalLoop** is a topic-based, continuously updated workspace that helps users stay informed, build expertise, and grow their online presence. A user creates a SignalLoop around a topic, adds sources over time, chats with an AI grounded in those sources, and receives a daily content-and-networking briefing each morning.

## Problem

Users who want to build authority in a subject spend significant time gathering information, extracting insights, creating social content, and identifying relevant people to engage with. Their research is fragmented and rarely turns into a consistent daily habit.

## Goals

- Make it simple to maintain a living research loop for a topic.
- Turn source material into useful daily audio and social content.
- Help users discover relevant people and engage with them thoughtfully.
- Keep generated insights traceable to the loop’s sources where possible.

## User Experience

1. The user creates a SignalLoop by entering a title, topic description, and initial sources, including links, documents, videos, or pasted text.
2. The user can add, remove, or refresh sources whenever needed.
3. The user chats with the SignalLoop to ask questions, synthesize ideas, compare sources, and draft content. Responses cite supporting sources.
4. Every morning, the SignalLoop generates a **Daily Briefing** containing:
   - An audio podcast digest of notable source updates and key ideas.
   - One editable tweet/X post designed to share a useful insight and strengthen the user’s voice on the topic.
   - A “person to know” profile: a relevant person found on LinkedIn or X/Twitter, including public context, recent themes, why they are relevant, and recommended ways to engage authentically.
5. The user can listen, edit or copy the post, save the person, dismiss recommendations, or provide feedback to improve future briefings.

## Requirements

| Area | Requirement |
|---|---|
| SignalLoop creation | Create a named SignalLoop with a topic description and one or more initial sources |
| Source management | Add sources on demand; show processing status, source metadata, and retrieval/citation support |
| Grounded chat | Enable conversational Q&A and synthesis based on SignalLoop sources, with citations |
| Daily scheduling | Deliver one Daily Briefing each morning at a user-selected local time |
| Audio digest | Generate a concise, listenable audio recap with a transcript and cited source references |
| Social post | Generate one topic-relevant, editable tweet/X post in the user’s selected tone |
| People discovery | Identify one relevant public LinkedIn or X/Twitter profile and provide engagement intelligence based on publicly available information |
| Feedback | Let users rate or dismiss each briefing item; use feedback to tune relevance and tone |

## Trust and Guardrails

- Clearly distinguish source-grounded claims from externally discovered public-profile information.
- Cite source material in chat, transcripts, and briefing summaries.
- Use only public professional information for people discovery; do not infer sensitive attributes or recommend spammy outreach.
- Frame engagement recommendations around genuine interaction, not automated or deceptive tactics.
- Require user review before a post is published; SignalLoop does not auto-post or auto-message people.

## Success Metrics

- Weekly active SignalLoops and source additions per SignalLoop.
- Daily Briefing open/listen rate and podcast completion rate.
- Percentage of generated social posts copied, edited, or published by users.
- Saves and engagement actions on recommended people.
- User-rated relevance of the daily digest, post, and person recommendation.
